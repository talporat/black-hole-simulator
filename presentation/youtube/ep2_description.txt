Bending Light in 200 Lines of C++ and OpenGL | Building a Black Hole Simulator, Part 2

------------------------------------------------------------

We turn the two equations from part 1 into a real program: light rays traced step by step around a black hole, and drawn with OpenGL from scratch. Every line of code is shown next to the maths it comes from.

In this video:
- Writing the equations of motion for light in C++
- Solving H = 0 with the quadratic formula
- Why Runge-Kutta 4 beats Euler's method
- OpenGL from zero: a window, two shaders, vertex buffers and draw calls
- Finding the black hole's shadow at 5.2 M

The code for this part: https://github.com/talporat/black-hole-simulator (folder stages/01_rays_2d).
