Ray Tracing a Black Hole on the GPU | Building a Black Hole Simulator, Part 3

------------------------------------------------------------

One ray of light for every pixel, a few hundred steps each, sixty times a second. We move the physics into a GLSL fragment shader and fly a camera around the black hole in real time.

In this video:
- How a ray tracer makes a picture, and why a black hole breaks it
- Why this needs the graphics card
- Covering the screen with a single triangle
- Porting the physics from C++ to GLSL
- Building a camera from two angles and cross products
- Reading the result: the shadow and the lensed sky

The code for this part: https://github.com/talporat/black-hole-simulator (folder stages/02_raytracer_3d).
