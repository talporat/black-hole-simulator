The Glowing Disk: Doppler, Redshift and Tone Mapping | Building a Black Hole Simulator, Part 4

------------------------------------------------------------

We give the black hole a disk of hot gas orbiting at half the speed of light, and explain the two most famous features of a black hole image: the halo, and the lopsided glow.

In this video:
- Why one flat disk shows up three times
- Finding where a ray crosses the disk
- Orbital speed from centripetal force and gravity
- Doppler shift and gravitational redshift in one number
- Floating-point textures, framebuffers and tone mapping in OpenGL

The code for this part: https://github.com/talporat/black-hole-simulator (folder stages/03_disk).
